# Hotel Selection Criteria

## Focus: Budget Hotels Only
- Price range: under INR 3,000/night (budget), INR 3,000–7,000 (mid-budget)
- Only include hotels rated 3.8/5 or higher with at least 100 reviews.

## Where to Search
- Booking.com: https://www.booking.com/

## Selection Priority
1. Value for money (best amenities at lowest price)
2. Rating score and number of reviews
3. Location (safe area, near transport or attractions)

## Key Features to Highlight
- Free WiFi
- AC rooms
- Breakfast included
- 24-hour check-in
- Clean bathrooms (commonly mentioned in reviews)
- Near metro/bus stand

## Review Summary Guidelines
- Summarise what most guests praise (e.g. "clean rooms, helpful staff")
- Mention any common complaint if significant (e.g. "no elevator")
- Keep guest summary to one short sentence
